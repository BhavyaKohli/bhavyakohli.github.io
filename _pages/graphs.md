<p>Small molecular graphs (2&ndash;6 atoms) drift in from the edges and settle into a spring layout as their atoms gently repel each other, with every bond pulling to the same length. Click an empty spot to spawn one at your cursor, or click a graph to nudge it toward its nearest neighbour. Nodes of different graphs are attracted to each other, so they drift together and fuse when two atoms come within reach, leaving the graphs hinged there as their atoms push one another apart until the fused molecule settles at a relaxed spacing; if another pair of nodes closes in, the graphs rotate about that hinge to snap the closest pair together and lock into one rigid molecule. Nodes and edges collide, and once a molecule has merged 3 times its atoms shift to a warm gold, flare for an instant, and fade out over the next second, replaced by a fresh graph where it stood.</p> -->
